Common questions

Why am I seeing jobs that don't suit me?

FlickRole learns from your swipes, so the feed improves as you use it. You can also update your target roles, locations and salary in Profile, or see and reset what the feed has learned in Profile → Tune My Feed.

How do I apply for a job?

Open a job and tap Apply. You'll go to the job site that posted it to finish your application. FlickRole keeps track of it under Applications.

Can I import my resume?

Yes. Import a resume or LinkedIn PDF during setup, or later from Profile → Job preferences → Edit, and FlickRole fills in your roles, skills and experience for you to review.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Profile → Delete Account. This permanently deletes your account and all its data. See our Privacy Policy for details.
