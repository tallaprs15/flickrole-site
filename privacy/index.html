Privacy Policy

Last updated 8 October 2026

What we collect

Your name and email address. If you sign up with a password, it's stored securely by our sign-in provider and we never see it. If you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive only your name and email (with Apple, you can hide your real email). The job preferences you enter: target roles, years of experience, locations, work modes, industries, skills and minimum salary. How you use job listings: jobs you pass on, save, apply to or hide, and which details you open.

How we use it

Only to run FlickRole: to sign you in, to show and rank jobs that suit you, and to keep your saved jobs and applications in sync across your devices. Your preferences also help decide which job searches we run each day. We don't sell your data, show ads, or share it with advertisers or data brokers.

Where it's stored

Your account and data are stored with Supabase in their Sydney, Australia region. The model that learns your job preferences runs on your device, and a copy of its history is kept there.

Resume import

If you import a resume or LinkedIn PDF, the file is sent to Google's Gemini API to read your details (roles, skills, experience, industries and location), which you then review. FlickRole doesn't store the file. It keeps only your job titles, employers, years and qualifications (never contact details), shown under Profile → Experience, where you can remove them at any time. Google processes the file under its paid API terms, which don't use it to train its models.

Job listings and applying

Job listings come from third-party job sites (currently Adzuna). When you open or apply to a job, you leave FlickRole for that site, and its own privacy policy applies.

Your choices

You can edit your preferences at any time in Profile, see or reset what your feed has learned in Profile → Tune My Feed, and permanently delete your account and all its data with Profile → Delete Account.

Contact

Questions or requests about your data: srikant.t@gmail.com. We handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles.
